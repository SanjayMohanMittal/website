Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

This site (sanjaymohanmittal.com) is a personal and professional page for Sañjay Mohan Mittal. It does not have a sign-up flow, a user account system, or any form that collects your information. This policy explains, plainly, what little data is involved when you visit.

What this site does not do

This site does not use tracking cookies, does not sell or share data with advertisers, and does not run any third-party ad network. There is no login, no account, and no database that stores anything you submit, because nothing on the site is submitted to it.

Analytics

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand overall traffic (which pages are visited, roughly how many people visit, and general referrer/device/country-level information). It is cookieless and does not track you individually across sites or store a persistent identifier tied to you. It cannot identify you personally.

Contact links

The email address, phone number, "Book a call," and "Get in touch" links on this site open your own email client or phone app (via mailto: and tel: links). Whatever you choose to send is sent directly from your device to sanjaymm@gmail.com the same way any email you write would be — this site itself does not see, store, or process that message.

External links

Children's privacy

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from any visitor.

Changes to this policy
