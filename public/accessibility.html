Accessibility Statement

Last updated August 2026

This site aims to be usable by everyone, including people using screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, screen magnification, or other assistive technology. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What's in place

  • A "skip to main content" link for keyboard users.
  • Semantic landmarks (navigation, main content, footer) for screen reader navigation.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators on every link, button, and interactive control.
  • Text and background colors checked to meet a 4.5:1 contrast ratio.
  • Descriptive alt text on every meaningful image; decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
  • The scrolling quotes section can be paused with a visible control, not just by hovering.
  • Motion and parallax effects are reduced automatically if your operating system has "reduce motion" turned on.
  • All content and controls are operable by keyboard alone.

Known limitations

This statement is honest about where the site may still fall short. If you hit a barrier not listed here, please report it — see below.

Third-party content

Book purchases happen on Amazon, which maintains its own accessibility standards independently of this site.

Reporting an issue

If any part of this site is difficult to use with assistive technology, please let us know at sanjaymm@gmail.com with the page and the issue you ran into. We'll work to address it.
